Skills · the lesson page, Grid view

This is the live app, not screenshots: both screens are running the demo at speakbetter.app/demo/skills, so everything we push shows up here. Scroll, tap and play inside either one. Pick a colour to switch both.

What’s new: the bar under the nav (Skills / colour, the progress ring, which lesson you’re on) stays pinned as you scroll; the colour’s subtitle and description now fold open under the player; the player’s controls are shallower; “This lesson” and “All lessons” are two tabs.

Recommendations - what would make it easier to pick up

Fix firstOpen on the next unwatched lesson

Every colour opens on lesson 1, even when you’ve finished it. A student coming back should land on the first lesson they haven’t watched, with a small “Continue” label, so they never have to hunt for their place.

Fix firstCount a lesson as watched when it’s watched

Right now a lesson is marked watched (and pays its XP) after five seconds on the page, even if the video never plays. That makes the progress ring flatter the student. Count it once most of the video has played, around 80%.

Quick winTap the progress ring to see all lessons

The ring in the pinned bar already says “4 of 10”. Tapping it should jump to the All lessons tab, so the bar is a way in as well as a readout.

Quick winShow each lesson’s length

The All lessons list shows a thumbnail, number and title. Adding “1:40” on each thumbnail tells a student whether it fits the minute they have before they tap.

Quick winShow the description once, open

The first time a student opens a colour, have “Figurative & Sensory” already unfolded so they read what the colour is; after that it stays folded, as it is now.

PolishNext lesson in the pinned bar

On a phone, after scrolling down to the notes, the only way to the next lesson is back up to the player. A small “Next ›” at the right of the pinned bar keeps it one tap away.

PolishName the buttons the first time

The player’s icons (captions, speed, portrait, next) are clear to us but not to a first-timer. On the first video, show their names under them for one play, then go back to icons only.

PolishCelebrate finishing a colour

When the ring closes at 10 of 10, fill it in the colour and say so in the bar (“Paint complete”), with a pointer to the next colour. Finishing should feel like a moment, not just a number.
